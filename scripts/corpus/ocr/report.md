# Quarterly Operations Report

## Summary

Revenue grew by twelve percent compared with the previous quarter, driven mainly by the new subscription plans. Operating costs stayed flat because the migration to the new data center finished on schedule.

## Regional results

| Region | Revenue | Customers | Change |
|--------|---------|-----------|--------|
| North | 1,204 | 318 | +8% |
| South | 986 | 271 | +15% |
| East | 1,552 | 402 | +11% |
| West | 743 | 199 | -3% |

## Next steps

1. Expand the support team in the West region.
2. Finish the pricing review before the end of the year.
3. Publish the customer satisfaction survey results.
